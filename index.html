The job

Your club, your calls

Quick seasons and clear choices, in the spirit of the classic football managers of the 90s.

Pick the team

Choose the formation and the mentality, then watch the match play out on the pitch.

Do the deals

Offer a fee to the club and a wage to the player. Every refusal tells you why.

Balance the books

Wages, gate money, the club shop and the bar. A well-run club can pay its way at every level.

Follow the world

Where the game stands

Matchnight already plays season after season: pick any club in the four English divisions, then chase promotion, cup runs and European nights while 128 nations play on around you. The latest builds brought young players who grow into their talent (one in ten never quite makes it), free agents who find new clubs, and a quicker way to play through the weeks. Next on the list are an easier way around the game's screens and the first match sounds and music.
