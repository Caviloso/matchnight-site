Story Mode

Everyone in town knows why he left. Except you.

You take over a small seaside club two days before the season. The last manager is gone, the town is lying badly, and the only person who will talk to you is the one you've been told to avoid. Your results and your choices write the story over eight seasons, and the secret gets heavier with every win.

Four managers said no. You're the fifth.

  1. WednesdayThe manager leaves at midnight, in the rain.
  2. ThursdayYou arrive. Nobody mentions it.
  3. SaturdayThe season starts. The derby, at home.

The job

Your club, your calls

Six jobs, one club. Each comes down to a few clear choices, so the seasons move quickly.

Pick the team

Choose the formation and the approach, then the penalty taker and the captain.

Match night

Watch it on the pitch, with a crowd that reacts to every chance, a commentator for the big moments and your fans' own chant. Or take the quick result.

Do the deals

Offer a fee to the club and a wage to the player. Every refusal tells you why.

Balance the books

Wages, gate money, the club shop and the bar. A well-run club can pay its way at every level.

Follow the world

128 nations, leagues abroad, cup runs, summer tournaments and award nights all season long.

Build a legacy

Every trophy you win gets its own engraved plate, and your club's history keeps every honour and record.

The pyramid

Start anywhere. Climb to Europe.

Pick any of the 92 clubs in the four English divisions. Win promotion, survive the drop, and chase a place on the continent.

Every club is set in a real town, with its own invented name, players and ground.

What's coming

Next on the team sheet

What we're building next, in roughly this order. Each step goes into the game when it's ready, and the order may change as we play-test.

On the bench

After that: more depth, then getting Matchnight ready for PC and Android.

  1. 1

    Records and legendsUp next

    The records of your career, and the players who made your club: legends, a hall of fame and a proper send-off for the greats. Your academy keeps track of every player it raised. They're keener to come home, and won't celebrate if they score against you. Old heroes get a headline when they face your club again. The trophy cabinet is sorted by metal, and a newly won plate shines.

  2. 2

    Reputation

    Every player, club, league and ground has a name: known in town, across the country, on the continent or around the world. Reputation sets wages and fees, so there are bargains out there nobody has heard of yet. As your club grows, better deals come in: naming rights for your ground, a kit deal and season tickets.

  3. 3

    Team chemistry

    A bar shows how well your side gels. Players who play together build partnerships, and new signings need time to settle in.

  4. 4

    Your backroom staff

    Hire an assistant, a coach, a physio and a scout. Each one is graded and does one clear job, and you choose which jobs your assistant takes off your hands. Then comes the festive rush: the pile-up of matches over Christmas and New Year, with only a couple of days between games. Tired legs cost points, so you rotate the squad and lean on your physio.

  5. 5

    In the spotlight

    Big moments that need your call, from the supporters' club to a row with the referee. Talk to fans, players, other managers, the press and your sponsors, and see yourself on the weekly front pages, written by reporters you'll get to know.

  6. 6

    Saturday night

    A highlights show after the weekend's matches, and live cup draws with the pundits.

  7. 7

    The boardroom

    A board with its own personality, takeovers that can turn your club upside down, and job interviews when you go after a new club.

  8. 8

    A new way to watch

    A 2D match view, so you can follow every move as it builds. More on this once the design is settled.

  9. 9

    Story Mode

    Eight seasons at a small seaside club, a manager who vanished the night before you arrived, and a town that knows why. Your results write the story, and every choice is remembered.

The new look

Crests, trophies and icons

Every club has a crest in its own colours, with an emblem from its town, and the icons have an engraved finish. Every trophy gets a plate of its own: gold, silver or bronze for the prizes at home, by level, diamond for the cups won abroad and platinum for the manager awards.

How it's made

One fan, with AI tools

Matchnight is made by one football fan, who designs the game and plays every build. The code, the art and the game's text are written with Claude, an AI from Anthropic. The sound, the music and the commentator's voice are made with ElevenLabs, and the Story Mode poster with Canva's AI. No AI runs inside the game itself: everything you see and hear is made, played and checked before it reaches you.

News

How it's going

15 October 2026

The floodlights come on

Matchnight has found its voice. A floodlit intro switches the stadium on before the title screen, music plays on the start screens, and every match has its own sounds, with a crowd that reacts to every chance and a commentator for the big moments. You can also tap any player, club or score to open its card, with full career histories, match reports and a search that finds anyone as you type.

14 October 2026

Where the game stands

Matchnight already plays season after season: pick any club in the four English divisions, then chase promotion, cup runs and European nights while 128 nations play on around you. The latest builds brought young players who grow into their talent (one in ten never quite makes it), free agents who find new clubs, and a quicker way to play through the weeks.

0.0.58.015 October 20263 added · 2 changed · 1 fixed

Added

  • Match commentary: one line under the pitch tells every moment of the match as it happens, from passes and shots to cards, VAR checks, half time and the shoot-out, in the classic manager style.
  • Text view: follow a match with just the two line-ups and the live stats instead of the pitch, with the commentary line and every sound. One button swaps between the two views at any moment, and the match keeps the same pace.
  • The groundwork for other languages: every word in the game now lives in one text file, ready for translations later.

Changed

  • The key hints on the top menu and the Next step bar are gone for a cleaner screen. The keys still work, and Keyboard shortcuts in the menu lists them.
  • A contract's last season is now picked from a drop-down.

Fixed

  • With the transfer windows switched off, the Transfers screen no longer fails to open during the season.
