Every match on the pitch

26 October 2026

Matches now play out on a 2D pitch. Every chance is acted out the way it happened, from through balls and crosses to counter-attacks and corners, and finished with a header, a volley or a tap-in while the keeper dives full stretch. The players run, jump for headers and celebrate, goals are replayed up close, and the pitch wears as the season goes on. The latest build made it all more natural: players run at real speeds, a tackle comes from someone close by, and a match at normal speed takes about seven and a half minutes. In a hurry? Fast, Highlights and the text view are still there.
