0.0.63.0Media 119 October 20265 added · 1 changed

0.0.62.0Staff19 October 20269 added · 2 changed

Added

  • Your staff: an assistant manager, a first-team coach, a physio and a chief scout, each with a rating, a wage and an age. Better staff help your players grow, get back on their feet and get scouted, and they cost more. Hire and replace them on the new Club › Staff page, where a monthly short list waits for each job.
  • A bigger club starts with better staff, so building yours up is part of the climb.
  • Your assistant runs the games you don't watch, and he can take on jobs you switch on: renewing key players, selling the ones you don't need, filling gaps, strengthening the side and picking youngsters on youth day.
  • He gets better with every season and every game he runs. Once he is among the best, a big club may want him as its manager: ask him to stay with a raise, or let him go for a fee and face him later.
  • Take your assistant with you when you move to a new club.
  • Tired legs all season: the days between matches count, so the festive run and midweek cup nights leave players tired, and rotating the squad matters.
  • Compare two players side by side, from any player card.
  • Sort the Team list by position, name, rating, energy, morale or form.
  • Save backups: each save keeps the one before it, so a damaged save can be opened from its backup.

Changed

  • New icons throughout the game, in the trophies' silver and gold.
  • Player names: two-word surnames stay whole, and there are more surnames from Mexico, Australia and the Nordic countries, Portugal, Brazil, Spain, France and Germany.

0.0.61.0Team chemistry18 October 20265 added · 2 changed · 1 fixed

Added

  • Team gel: every side now has a gel from 0 to 100, shown as a bar over the pitch on the Squad screen. Half of it is how settled your eleven are at the club, half how well the players in each line know each other.
  • Gel changes results: a side that has played together for a season plays a little better, and a team of new faces starts a little weaker, so a big summer of signings costs a few points before it pays off.
  • New signings settle in: a player from your own country feels at home after about eight games; one from abroad takes a little longer, a team-mate from his country helps, and a player returning to a club he knows settles sooner. The squad list and the pitch show who is still settling in.
  • Partnerships: players in the same line build a partnership game by game, drawn on the pitch, and it is whole after a season together.
  • The next-match card compares the two sides' gel, and How to play explains it all.

Changed

  • When you make an offer, the wage box now opens at what the player asks, not above it.
  • More invented names: record holders, legends and grounds that shared a name with a real person have new ones.

Fixed

  • A career loaded when the game starts now keeps its own wages and numbers.
0.0.60.0Reputation18 October 20266 added · 1 changed

Added

  • Everything in the game now has a reputation: players, clubs, leagues, countries, towns, grounds and cups, from Unknown to World. It grows with a player's rating, the stage he plays on, caps, awards, goals and form, and fades slowly, so stars stay famous for a while after their best days.
  • Fame has a price: little-known players cost less and ask for lower wages, famous names cost more. Find players has a new "Hidden gems" sort to dig out bargains.
  • Players weigh up your club: a player better known than your club asks for more, and a star far above it won't talk until your club has grown.
  • Kit deals: when your deal runs out, three kit makers bid in pre-season: a big fixed fee, a share of shirt sales, or a long safe deal.
  • Naming rights: now and then a sponsor offers good money to put its name on your ground. Say yes and the fans grumble for a while; say no and the old name stays.
  • Season tickets: set the discount on Club, Ground & tickets. Holders keep the crowd up in a bad run, and a full ground stays full.

Changed

  • Careers saved in an older version carry on: every club and player gets a reputation when the career is loaded.

  • 64 more nations, 128 in all, with player names in their own languages, flags and their real past finals.

  • A league of nations for Europe's 54 national teams, in four divisions, with promotion, relegation and finals in the summer.
  • New International screens: qualifying, Europe's league of nations, tournaments, the history of all seven tournaments and the world ranking.

0.0.33.0New European cups11 October 20265 added · 4 changed · 1 fixed

Added

  • The European cups have their new shape: 36 clubs each in one league-phase table, then knockout play-offs, two-legged ties and a final.
  • 80 guest clubs from 24 more countries play in Europe.
  • The Super Cup in August: the Champions Cup winners against the Challenge Cup winners.
  • European prize money for taking part, for every win and draw, and for each round reached.
  • Scouting is automatic and free: your scout files six reports a week and sends you the pick of them once a month.

Changed

  • The Premier Division rests in European weeks and plays midweeks of its own instead.
  • Auto-pick keeps players in their own positions and says what it changed.
  • Commercial income every week, more TV money in the Second and Third Divisions, and a Finances screen that shows an average week.
  • Older careers finish this season's European cups the old way; the new shape starts next season.

Fixed

  • The "club is in the red" message no longer comes in the wrong week.
0.0.32.0Real calendar11 October 20265 added · 2 changed · 2 fixed

Added

  • Every match has a date: the season runs from August to May, with midweek rounds, Boxing Day, 28 December and New Year's Day.
  • The cups have their own days, with Sunday finals and the European cups on Wednesday and Thursday nights.
  • International breaks: the Premier Division stops while the other divisions play on.
  • Transfer windows on the real dates: summer until 1 September, winter through January, and free moves agreed from 1 January.
  • Derby day: the league round with the most derbies is played on Boxing Day.

Changed

  • Seasons show as 2026/27, and the news, awards and history use real months and dates.
  • Older careers finish this season on the old weeks and start the next on the real calendar.

Fixed

  • The fixture list no longer shows your current cup opponent against every later round.
  • Long club names no longer run into the home and away column of the fixture list.
