    The World Shield · Qualifier · Prototype

    Six phases, 15 minutes each. Each phase: 3 plays and 2 redraws. Basic cards build Momentum (the floodlights). Big moments spend it. Cards only work in the lit zones at their bottom. Get the ball into their box to shoot. Tackles can be fouls. Two yellows or a straight red and that player's cards leave your deck.

    Small club. Big nights. A first playable test of the Matchnight card game.
