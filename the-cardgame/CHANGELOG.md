# Matchnight: The Card Game, build history

Every build has a version number in the same style as Matchnight itself (vA.B.C.D: C goes up for a new feature
version, D for fixes on it). Every build is kept in [builds/](builds/), so any old version can still be opened.

| Version | Date | What changed |
|---|---|---|
| v0.0.3.5 | 4 Nov 2026 | The match fits the screen: no scrolling, End phase and Redraw always visible. Paintings fitted to the open space on the card (4:3). |
| v0.0.3.4 | 4 Nov 2026 | Card style C (Kevin's pick on the sample page): full art everywhere, the painting turns one colour in hand and deck, full colour when played or picked. |
| v0.0.3.3 | 4 Nov 2026 | Full-art cards: the picture fills the card, name bar at the top, dark glass text panel at the bottom. |
| v0.0.3.2 | 4 Nov 2026 | First painted card art in the picture window (Thunderbolt, Last-ditch slide). |
| v0.0.3.1 | 4 Nov 2026 | Real trading-card frames (name bar, cost gem, art window, type line, rarity gem, set number x/22, frame colour per kind, foil on rare and legend), player cards, VS card, 8-stop road, comic result, pack-flip and call-up screens, "Called up!" instead of "Signed!". |
| v0.0.3.0 | 4 Nov 2026 | The full run: pick 1 of 8 nations, 2 qualifiers, a group of 4, quarter, semi and final, shootouts, pick 1 of 3 cards after wins, a new player after the group and a legend before the final, album x/22, run saved in the browser. |
| v0.0.2.0 | 3 Nov 2026 | Table layout: your half at the bottom, the opponent's at the top, players as cards. |
| v0.0.1.0 | 3 Nov 2026 | First playable match (Spain vs Germany, side-on pitch strip). |

Also kept: [card-style-samples-2026-11-04.html](builds/card-style-samples-2026-11-04.html), the page Kevin used to pick card style C.
